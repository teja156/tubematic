# Strange Sight, Hidden Cause

`formula_id`: `strange-sight`

Open on a strange act or object in the middle of happening, give the cause in the second sentence, chain what follows, turn once, and stop on the most specific detail.

## Length

70 to 150 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| The sight | 0 to 15% | The strange thing in the middle of happening. The why is held back. |
| Cause | 15 to 45% | Why it is done or why it happens, plainly. |
| So | 45 to 55% | What followed from it. |
| But | 55 to 75% | The cost, or the second fact that turns it. |
| Verdict or number | 75 to 100% | The most specific detail, then stop. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Scene in motion (`scene`) | [Strange act, in progress]. |
| Anomaly (`anomaly`) | Most [group] [normal limit]. [This one] does not. |
| Flat claim (`claim`) | This is [thing]: [vivid description] that [what it does]. |

## What holds the viewer

The sight is on screen before it is explained, so the cause answers a question the viewer already has. The single turn in the second half gives a short script a second fact to wait for.

## Ending

One line that turns the opening's feeling around, or the most specific number. Nothing after it.

## Pitfalls

- Explaining before showing
- More than one turn
- A closing moral
- Padding past the band: this formula is short

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> [Strange act, in the middle of happening]. This is because [reason]. [Second reason]. So [consequence]. But [cost or hidden fact]. And [the most specific detail].
