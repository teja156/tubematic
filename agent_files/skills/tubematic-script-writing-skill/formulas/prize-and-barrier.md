# Prize and Barrier

`formula_id`: `prize-and-barrier`

Open on the prize as a number, keep it in view, and hold the barrier back to about two thirds so it lands as the reason.

## Length

150 to 240 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| The prize | 0 to 16% | The size of it as a number, against something the viewer knows. |
| However | 16 to 20% | One line: getting it is not easy. |
| The lucky exception | 20 to 40% | Where it already works, and why there. End on the tease as one image. |
| The more, the better | 40 to 70% | What improves as the effort grows, with a number. |
| The barrier | 70 to 90% | The cost or the limit that grows with it, as a number. |
| Who is trying | 90 to 100% | Who is working on it, or what would have to change. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Number (`number`) | [Untapped thing] holds [huge number]. Just [tiny fraction] of it could [benefit]. |
| Question (`question`) | If [prize] is right there, why is nobody using it? |
| Paradox (`paradox`) | [Thing] is everywhere and almost free, and we still cannot [use it]. |

## What holds the viewer

The prize stays in view while the barrier is held back, so the viewer keeps asking why not. The exception shows it is possible, which makes the barrier a real answer when it comes.

## Ending

Who is trying to change it, or what would have to change. No promise about when.

## Pitfalls

- Opening on the cost or the difficulty
- Reasons joined by 'also' with no single barrier
- A prize with no number
- A forecast stated as fact

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> [Untapped thing] holds [huge number]. Just [tiny fraction] could [benefit]. But getting to it is not easy. [One place where it works]. [The tease as one image]. The more [x], the better: [number]. The problem is [barrier], because [the cost that grows]. [Who is trying].
