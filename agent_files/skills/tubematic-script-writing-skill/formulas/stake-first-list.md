# Stake-First List

`formula_id`: `stake-first-list`

The stake first, then the question and the count, then three items with one concrete tool or number each, the least expected one last.

## Length

130 to 200 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| Stake | 0 to 20% | The pain or the figure that makes the list necessary. |
| Question and count | 20 to 28% | How to reverse it, and how many things there are. Say the last one is the odd one. |
| Items | 28 to 90% | One vivid name and one concrete tool or number each, the least expected last. |
| The odd one, explained | 90 to 100% | Why the last item holds. Stop there. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| The viewer's problem (`problem`) | You will not [goal] while [pain]. So how do you [reverse it]? There are three things. |
| Number (`number`) | [N] things make [X] [quality]. The last one sounds wrong. |
| Question (`question`) | Why is [X] so [quality] when [similar thing] is not? |

## What holds the viewer

The stake makes the list worth sitting through, the count tells the viewer how far along they are, and the promise about the last item holds them to the end.

## Ending

The last item and why it holds, then stop.

## Pitfalls

- The bare list label as the opening
- Items of equal weight with nothing saved for last
- A summary or an ask after the last item
- An item with no tool, number or example

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> [Pain or figure]. So how do you [reverse it]? There are three things. One: [vivid name]. [Concrete tool or number]. Two: [vivid name]. [Concrete]. And the odd one: [vivid name]. [Concrete]. It sounds wrong, but [why it holds].
