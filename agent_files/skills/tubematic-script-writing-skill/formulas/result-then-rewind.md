# Result First, Then Rewind

`formula_id`: `result-then-rewind`

Open on the end state, ask what happened, name the culprit or the choice early, and prove it with pairs of numbers.

## Length

150 to 230 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| End state | 0 to 15% | The striking result first: the fall as a number, or the two extremes of one life. |
| What happened | 15 to 25% | The question, then the culprit or the choice, named. |
| Rewind | 25 to 55% | Two or three dated facts, in order. |
| Proof number | 55 to 75% | One figure against a benchmark the viewer knows. |
| Consequence pair | 75 to 90% | From A to B: the before and the after as numbers. |
| Verdict | 90 to 100% | One line that judges the choice. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Result first (`result`) | Remember [product]? [What it was]. [Its key figure] is down [N percent]. |
| Both ends named (`frame`) | [Person] was [one extreme] in [year] and [the other extreme] by [year]. |
| Question (`question`) | How does [a company that had everything] end up [end state]? |
| Flat claim (`claim`) | [Familiar product] exists because of [one surprising fact]. |

## What holds the viewer

The viewer knows how it ends and stays for the cause. Naming the culprit early turns the middle into evidence, and every fact carries a date or a number.

## Ending

The before-and-after pair, or one line that judges the choice. No lesson.

## Pitfalls

- Telling it in date order from the beginning
- No named person, choice or culprit
- Figures with nothing to compare them to
- An invented figure: every number has a source

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> Remember [X]? [What it meant]. [Its figure] is down [N percent]. So what happened? [One culprit or one choice]. In [year], [fact]. In [year], [fact]. [Proof number], more than [benchmark]. That turned [A] into [B] in [time]. [Verdict].
