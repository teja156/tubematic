# News Take

`formula_id`: `news-take`

The event with its actor and its irony in the first sentence, why it matters to the viewer, the biggest claim, the evidence for it, the catch, and a take.

## Length

170 to 240 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| What happened | 0 to 12% | The event with its date, its actor and the irony or the opponent. A date, never 'last week': the video may be posted later. |
| Why it matters to you | 12 to 25% | What it changes for the viewer. |
| The biggest claim | 25 to 35% | In plain terms. |
| On the surface, yes | 35 to 55% | The evidence that the claim holds. |
| The catch | 55 to 80% | One detail from a source that limits it. |
| The take | 80 to 100% | Who gains, who loses, or the honest limits. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Scene in motion (`scene`) | On [date], [actor] got so tired of [practice] that [surprising act]. |
| Flat claim (`claim`) | [Actor] just showed that your [thing you own] [surprising fact]. |
| Paradox (`paradox`) | [Actor] spent [years] building [X]. On [date] it [did the opposite]. |

## What holds the viewer

The event is in the first sentence, so nothing is spent on backstory. The viewer stays to learn whether the claim is true, and the catch re-opens it once the evidence seems to settle it.

## Ending

The take, or the honest limits of the news. No forecast stated as fact.

## Pitfalls

- Opening on the backstory
- Leaving the event after promising it
- A claim or a catch with no source
- News the audience has already seen: it dates within days

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> On [date], [actor] [did surprising thing], with [one irony]. This matters to you because [what it changes]. In plain terms, [the biggest claim]. Is it true? On the surface, yes: [evidence]. But [the catch, from a source]. [The take].
