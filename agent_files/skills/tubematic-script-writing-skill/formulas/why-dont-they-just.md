# Why Don't They Just

`formula_id`: `why-dont-they-just`

Name the obvious fix the viewer would suggest, then the rule of the system that blocks it, the exception that proves the rule, and what the rule means.

## Length

150 to 230 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| The obvious fix | 0 to 8% | The question as the viewer would ask it: why not just do the obvious thing? |
| Catch-up and irony | 8 to 25% | One clause on who or what this is, for a newcomer, plus an irony that sharpens the question. |
| Re-ask | 25 to 30% | The question again, shorter. |
| The rule | 30 to 60% | The rule that blocks the fix, with its source and one concrete picture of it at work. |
| Proof by exception | 60 to 85% | Who or what managed it anyway, and what made that case different. |
| The rule as meaning | 85 to 100% | What the rule says about the system, or one dry line that answers the first question again. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Question (`question`) | Why don't [they] just [obvious fix]? |
| Anomaly (`anomaly`) | [Something odd in the design or on the map]. By every obvious rule it should have [changed long ago]. |
| Flat claim (`claim`) | [Obvious fix] would end [problem] overnight. Nobody does it. |

## What holds the viewer

One question that gets sharper each time and is never merely repeated. A short answer may come at once if it stays incomplete. The re-ask and the exception re-open it; refusing the next obvious step ('surely then...? No.') is a third.

## Ending

The rule restated as meaning, or a dry one-sentence kicker with the present-day state.

## Pitfalls

- Context before the question
- An open debate with no verdict
- An obscure detail as the subject: the obscure detail has to be the answer
- A question opening under a publish title that asks the same question: title the video with the rule or the exception, and let the opening ask

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> Why don't [they] just [obvious fix]? [Subject] is [one-clause identity], and yet [irony]. So why not? Because [rule], as [source] shows. In practice, [one concrete picture of the rule at work]. Only [named exception] manages it, because [what is different]. For everyone else, [the rule restated as meaning].
