# Contender, Then Champion

`formula_id`: `contender-champion`

Ask a superlative question, score a plausible candidate out loud, then bring in the winner nobody guessed and count past the candidate.

## Length

160 to 220 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| Question | 0 to 5% | The superlative, asked in one sentence. |
| The contender, scored | 5 to 35% | Item by item, to a total. |
| Turn | 35 to 40% | One line: that is still not the most. |
| The champion's case | 40 to 90% | What counts against it first, then its tally, passing the contender. |
| Final score | 90 to 100% | The number, and that it is the most. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Question (`question`) | Which [thing] has the most [measure]? [Plausible contender] is a contender. |
| Number (`number`) | [Contender] has [N] [measure]. That is not the record. |
| Flat claim (`claim`) | The [thing] with the most [measure] is not [the one you would guess]. |

## What holds the viewer

The contender's score gives the viewer a number to beat, and it stays in view while the champion's tally climbs past it.

## Ending

The final score. Nothing after it.

## Pitfalls

- Facts in date order with no contender
- Naming the champion before scoring the contender
- A tally the viewer cannot follow item by item
- An invented count

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> Which [thing] has the most [measure]? You would guess [candidate]: [tally], which makes [N]. But that is still not the most. Despite [handicap], [winner]: [tally, with one complication]. That makes [M], the most in the world.
