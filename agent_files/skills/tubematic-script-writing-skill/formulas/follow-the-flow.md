# Follow the Flow

`formula_id`: `follow-the-flow`

One question tied to something the viewer does, then the parts in the order things happen, each term named only after its job, closed by a callback to the action.

## Length

90 to 260 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| The action | 0 to 8% | The viewer does the thing, and the first step is already moving. |
| Stage 1 | 8 to 28% | The first part, in the order the system itself works, with a count if there is one. |
| Stages 2 to 4 | 28 to 80% | One or two sentences each. What it does first, its name second. One contrast or one corrected misconception on the way. |
| Last stage | 80 to 92% | The most vivid or most surprising stage, kept for last. |
| Callback | 92 to 100% | Back to the action the script opened on, in one clause. Then stop. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Question (`question`) | What really happens when you [everyday action]? |
| Scene in motion (`scene`) | You [do the everyday action]. Before [short time] is up, [the first thing] has already [happened]. |
| Number (`number`) | [Everyday action] takes [a count] of [steps or hops] and about [a short time]. |

## What holds the viewer

The count or the path is the progress bar: the viewer always knows how far the end is. The pull is 'what happens next to me', so no extra question has to be made up. The biggest obstacle or the strangest stage goes last.

## Ending

The last stage's detail, or the callback to the action. Never a summary of the stages.

## Pitfalls

- Ordering by textbook category
- Name, then definition, then example, repeated for every part
- Stopping the flow for a background lesson
- A question opening under a publish title that asks the same question: title the video with the surprising stage, and let the opening ask

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> What really happens when you [action]? It starts when [first thing]. First, [part] [does its job]: that is [its name]. Next, [part 2] [checks or transforms]. Many people think [misconception]; in fact [fact]. Then [part 3] [hands over]. Last, [the final stage, its most vivid detail]. Every time you [action].
