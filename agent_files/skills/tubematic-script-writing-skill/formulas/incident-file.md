# Incident File

`formula_id`: `incident-file`

Start on the moment of failure or its price. Rewind to an ordinary person making a reasonable wrong call, raise an alarm at every beat with one sentence of mechanism each, then the cause and the cost.

## Length

190 to 240 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0 to 8% | The fatal action said casually, or the price as a number. |
| Rewind | 8 to 22% | A time stamp, the organisation in one line, and an unnamed person (by role) about to make the call. |
| Alarm 1 and the wrong call | 22 to 40% | The first symptom and a reasonable wrong assumption. |
| Alarm 2 | 40 to 60% | It gets worse. One sentence explains the one mechanism the story needs here. |
| The act | 60 to 70% | The spoken re-opening: the fix they reached for, or the fatal action again, now understood. |
| Cause | 70 to 85% | A chain of three steps. |
| Before and after | 85 to 95% | The number pair: what it was, what it became. |
| Kicker | 95 to 100% | One dry line. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Number (`number`) | Imagine [losing a large number] by [one tiny action]. [Organisation] nearly did. |
| Spoken line (`quote`) | '[A casual line announcing the fatal action]. It should be safe.' |
| Scene in motion (`scene`) | At [time] on [date], [a person, by role] [ran one routine thing], and within [short time] [the symptoms]. |

## What holds the viewer

The viewer knows the ending and the people in the story do not. A number in every beat. Facts come in the order an investigation finds them (effect, then cause, then the contradiction), never in calendar order. A spoken re-opening at about three fifths.

## Ending

The before-and-after pair, then one dry kicker. No list of lessons.

## Pitfalls

- Inventing an incident or its numbers: every figure needs a source
- Telling it from the victims' side
- Ending on 'the lesson is'

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> [The price, or the fatal line]. [Organisation] was [doing X] with [tool], [one-clause gloss]. At [time], [first symptom]. [The person, by role] assumed [a reasonable wrong cause]. Then [second symptom, with a number]. [The mechanism, in one sentence]. The fix? [One sentence], because [cause]. After that, [metric] went from [before] to [after]. [Kicker].
