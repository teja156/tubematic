# Paradox, Mechanism, Reframe

`formula_id`: `paradox-mechanism-reframe`

Two true facts about a thing the viewer owns that cannot both be true. Give the answer in one line early, show how it works, and end by turning the opening belief upside down.

## Length

150 to 280 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| Paradox | 0 to 7% | Two facts about the viewer's own object that seem incompatible, in one sentence. |
| Question and one-line answer | 7 to 15% | Ask what it is really doing and answer in one line: not the thing the viewer assumed, this other thing. The what is given; the how is held back. |
| Scene that will fail | 15 to 20% | One everyday scene with the viewer in it, which the mechanism will break later. |
| Mechanism | 20 to 55% | Four to six steps, one concrete noun in each. One real number or name in the whole script. |
| Turn | 55 to 65% | 'But': the scene from the third beat fails, and the mechanism is the reason. |
| That is also why | 65 to 82% | Two more things the viewer has lived through that the same mechanism explains. |
| Reframe | 82 to 100% | Turn the opening belief upside down in two sentences, then stop. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Paradox (`paradox`) | [Your thing] [does surprising A], and [fails at obvious B]. |
| Question (`question`) | [Every part of it] is [a handicap], and it still [works] almost every time. So how? |
| Anomaly (`anomaly`) | [Organisation or system] [does something that looks like throwing value away], every day. |

## What holds the viewer

Two questions are open inside the first fifth: the paradox, and a scene that has not failed yet. Because the answer arrives early, the viewer stays for the how. The question re-opens at the scene, at the turn and at the first 'also why'.

## Ending

The reframe: the belief the paradox planted, stated the other way round as a fact. No recap of the mechanism and no lesson.

## Pitfalls

- Naming the mechanism's technical term before the viewer has seen it work
- Ending on a summary of the mechanism where the turned-around belief belongs
- A paradox nobody has felt

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> [Thing you own] [does surprising A], and [ignores obvious B]. So what is it doing? It is not [what you assumed], it is [the real mechanism, one line]. Picture [an everyday scene that will fail]. [Step]. [Step]. [Step]. [Step]. But [the scene fails], so [consequence]. That is also why [something you have lived]. And why [a second one]. So [your thing] never [what you assumed]. It [the real mechanism].
