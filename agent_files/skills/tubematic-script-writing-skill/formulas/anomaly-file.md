# Anomaly File

`formula_id`: `anomaly-file`

State the rule, show the one case that breaks it, say why that should be impossible, then investigate: how it worked, the new evidence, the answer with numbers, and one detail that still does not fit.

## Length

150 to 450 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| The rule | 0 to 8% | What normally happens, and why that makes sense. | - |
| The break | 8 to 16% | The opposite, as one picture. | - |
| Why impossible | 16 to 20% | One short line of stakes: things like this do not just happen. | - |
| How it worked | 20 to 40% | The system in plain numbers. | - |
| New evidence | 40 to 55% | Dated: when it was found, and what was unexpected. | a re-opening: a visible change on its first word |
| Answer | 55 to 80% | The hypothesis as a question, then yes, with the mechanism and two numbers. | the payoff: the video's strongest picture |
| Reopen | 80 to 95% | One piece of evidence still does not fit. | a re-opening: a visible change on its first word |
| Close | 95 to 100% | The open question, stated plainly, or the current theory in one sentence. | - |

A segment's `visual` brief for a beat with an on-screen duty names the change it asks for. The scene author reads the same duty from the timeline.

## First frame

The rule and the exception side by side, with the odd one marked. Segment 1's `visual` brief asks for this picture, already moving, and it is the subject of the first sentence.

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Anomaly (`anomaly`) | When [this kind of thing] happens, [the pattern] almost always follows. In [case], [the opposite picture]. |
| Question (`question`) | What happened to [the object]? And what happened to [the people]? |
| Scene in motion (`scene`) | In [year], [a person, by role] opened [the thing] and found [what should not have been there]. |

## What holds the viewer

Stacked questions: the first answer lands at about three fifths and a new question opens before the viewer can leave. The dated new evidence re-opens the middle; an answer with numbers that seems to close the case comes before the reopen.

## Ending

An open question, stated plainly, or the current theory in one sentence. It must be a real open question.

## Pitfalls

- Inventing the detail that does not fit: the reopen must be true
- Skipping the rule, so the break means nothing

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> When [this kind of thing] happens, [the normal finding]. [Why that makes sense]. But in [case], [the opposite, one picture]. [Things like this] do not just [do that]. [How it worked, two numbers]. Then in [year], [someone, by role] found [new fact]. Was it [hypothesis]? Yes: [mechanism, numbers]. But one detail still does not fit: [second anomaly]. [The open question].
