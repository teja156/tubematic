# Scheme Machine

`formula_id`: `scheme-machine`

A true scheme told in the third person: the result first, the scheme as a loop, the one number or one sentence that makes it work, the tell, and a second layer in the last line.

## Length

150 to 210 words across every segment's `voice`. Stay inside the band: the beats below are sized for it.

## Beats

In this order. A share is the part of the script's words the beat covers; hold each within a few points.

| Beat | Share of the script | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| The result | 0 to 15% | Who got what from whom, as a number. |
| The offer at full strength | 15 to 30% | Why it looked legitimate, or the rule being used. |
| The machine | 30 to 50% | The loop in one sentence, three or four steps, the last feeding the first. |
| The trick and the tell | 50 to 68% | The one number or one mechanism sentence that makes it work, then one concrete detail anyone could have spotted. |
| Scale | 68 to 85% | The total, as a number against something the viewer knows. |
| Second layer | 85 to 100% | The motive, the multiplier or the outcome. The last line. |

## Openings

Draft one opening from each of three shapes. The type goes in `hooks[].type`.

| Type (`type`) | Shape |
|---|---|
| Result first (`result`) | [An ordinary person, by role] built [a machine for free money] and took [amount], [legally or unnoticed]. |
| Paradox (`paradox`) | [A crew] took [amount] from [institution] without ever [doing the obvious crime]. |
| Number (`number`) | [A fee of N percent] against [M percent back]. That gap paid [someone] [amount]. |

## What holds the viewer

The result is given and the method is held back. The method then arrives as a machine with one number at its heart, and the viewer asks 'would I have spotted it?'. The trick lands in the middle; what waits for the end is a second layer.

## Ending

The outcome, the motive or the multiplier. No list of ways to protect yourself.

## Pitfalls

- Invented first-person testimony
- Opening on the verdict
- Ending on a warning list
- Numbers without a source

## Template

The shape of the whole script with the facts left out. Fill it with this topic's facts; never keep its wording.

> [A person, by role] took [amount] from [institution or victims], [legally or unnoticed]. It looked legitimate because [the offer at full strength]. Behind it: [step 1], then [step 2], then [step 3], which fed [step 1]. It worked because [the cost] was smaller than [the gain]. The giveaway was [one concrete detail]. By the end, [the scale against a benchmark]. And [the second layer].
